Frog Cleaner

Privacy Policy · Free. Simple. Respectful.

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Our promise

Frog Cleaner is free to use. It has no ads, subscriptions, accounts, analytics, or tracking. I do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Information the app can access

Frog Cleaner only accesses files in folders you choose through Android, or shared storage after you explicitly enable Full device analysis in Android Settings. It may read file names, locations, sizes, and last-modified dates to show you a storage review.

Exact Duplicate Finder reads complete file contents only on your device to calculate comparison hashes. Photo and video previews are created locally on your device. File contents, names, locations, comparison hashes, and previews are not uploaded.

Why storage and accessibility permissions are requested

Folder access lets Frog Cleaner scan only the folders you select. Full device analysis is optional and uses Android's special all-files-access setting to review shared storage. Android continues to protect other apps' private data and protected Android folders. Frog Cleaner never enables this access automatically.

If you choose to use automated batch cache or storage clearing, Frog Cleaner requires you to manually enable an Android Accessibility Service. This service is strictly used on-device to automate screen taps within Android System Settings to clear temporary files for selected apps. It does not read, log, collect, or transmit any on-screen text, personal data, or user input.

Information kept on your device

The app stores chosen folder references and any file or folder exclusions you create so those choices continue to work. Temporary preview files may be kept in Frog Cleaner's cache and can be cleared from Settings. Android backup for this app is disabled.

File and cleaning actions

You choose every file action. Frog Cleaner does not select files automatically. Permanent deletion cannot be undone. Eligible photos, videos, and audio can be sent to Android Trash after you approve Android's own confirmation screen; Android manages their retention and restoration.

For automated cache or storage cleaning, you explicitly select which apps to queue. You can pause or stop the process at any time, and no system settings are altered outside of the specific app cleanup actions you initiate.

Other apps and external links

When you choose Open with another app, Android gives that app temporary access to the file you selected. Frog Cleaner does not control that app's privacy practices.

The optional Send Me a Coffee button opens Buy Me a Coffee in your browser. Buy Me a Coffee has its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If Frog Cleaner's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.
